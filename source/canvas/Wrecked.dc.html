Why a wreck still pays

A crash wrecks the plastic, not the whole bike.

Most crashes take out the fairings, the bars, a lever or two and maybe the front end. The engine, wheels, suspension, wiring and a hundred smaller parts often come through fine. Riders all over the country need those parts to keep their own machines running.

So when a body shop or dealer says it's not worth fixing, that's usually where we come in. Your offer is based on what's still good, not on what's broken.

Parts that often survive a crash

  • Engine and transmission
  • Wheels and brakes
  • Rear shock and swingarm
  • Wiring harness and ECU
  • Gauges and switches
  • Exhaust and seat

Titles and paperwork

What paperwork does a wrecked bike need?

Clean title

Easiest case. Have it at pickup and we'll sign it over together.

Salvage title

A Florida salvage title is still a title and can be transferred. Have it on hand at pickup.

Certificate of destruction

That means the bike is meant to be taken apart, which is what we do anyway. Send us a photo of the certificate and we'll go over it with you.

Insurance totaled it, you kept it

In Florida the insurance company gets the salvage paperwork issued and then reassigns it back to you. Still waiting on it? Text us anyway and we'll plan around it.

Lost the title

Let's talk. Send us whatever you do have, like an old registration or bill of sale, and a photo of the VIN.

Every time

We check the VIN before we buy and write up a signed bill of sale. We don't buy stolen machines.

Florida title rules come from the FLHSMV. This is general info, not legal advice.
