Questions

What sellers ask us

I lost the title. Can I still sell it?

Let's talk. Lots of older bikes have lost their paperwork over the years. We buy for parts, so we can often still make it work. We'll ask for whatever you do have, like a bill of sale or an old registration, and we check the VIN on every bike before we buy it. We don't buy stolen machines, period.

Do you buy bikes that don't run?

Most of what we buy doesn't run. Seized engines, dead electrics, wrecks and long-term barn sitters are all fine.

How do I get paid?

Cash, handed to you when we pick it up. No checks to wait on.

Do you charge for pickup?

There's no separate fee and no surprise at the curb. The cost of coming to get it is already figured into the offer we give you, so the number we quote is the cash you get.

How soon can you pick it up?

Usually within 48 hours of you accepting the offer. We'll text you a time that works.

I have more than one. Will you take them all?

Absolutely. Multi-bike pickups are our favorite, and we'll travel farther for them.
