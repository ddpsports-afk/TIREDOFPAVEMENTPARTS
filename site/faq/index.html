Selling

How do I sell my motorcycle without listing it?

Sell it directly to a local cash buyer. Text us a photo of the bike with the year, make, model and your town. We send back a cash offer. If you accept, we come pick it up and pay you on the spot. No ad, no photos for strangers, no negotiating.

What's the fastest way to sell a motorcycle, dirt bike or ATV?

Selling to a direct buyer is usually fastest, because there's no waiting on someone to find your listing. With us it's one text to start, one offer, and a pickup time you choose.

How do you decide what to offer?

We look at the year, make, model and condition, and at which parts are still good and in demand. Because we part bikes out, a machine that won't run can still have plenty of value.

Do I have to accept the offer?

No. Every offer is no-obligation. If it doesn't work for you, just say so.

Do I need to clean it, fix it or drain anything first?

No. Leave it exactly as it is. We buy bikes as-is, flat tires and all.

Titles & paperwork

I lost the title. Can I still sell my motorcycle or ATV?

Let's talk. Plenty of older bikes have lost their paperwork over the years, and because we buy for parts, we can often still make it work. We'll ask for whatever you do have, like a bill of sale, an old registration or a receipt, and we check the VIN before we buy.

Do you check whether a bike is stolen?

Yes. We run the VIN on every bike before we buy it, and every purchase gets a signed bill of sale. It protects you, it protects us, and it keeps stolen bikes out of the parts market.

I have the title. What do I do with it?

Have it on hand at pickup and we'll walk you through signing it over.

Do I keep my license plate in Florida?

Yes. In Florida the plate stays with you, not the vehicle. Take it off before we load the bike, or we can pull it for you at pickup.

Pickup & payment

How do I get paid?

Cash, in your hand, when we pick up the bike.

Do you charge for pickup?

There's no separate fee and no surprise at the curb. The cost of coming to get it is already figured into the offer we give you, so the number we quote is the cash you get. We pick up anywhere within 30 miles of Keystone Heights.

What areas do you cover?

Clay, Bradford, Putnam, Alachua and Union counties, including Keystone Heights, Starke, Middleburg, Green Cove Springs, Interlachen, Palatka, Melrose, Hawthorne, Gainesville and Lake Butler. Farther out? Text us anyway. We'll make the drive for the right bike.

Will you take more than one bike?

Absolutely. Multi-bike pickups are our favorite, and we'll travel beyond our usual area for them.

What we buy

What kinds of vehicles do you buy?

Motorcycles, dirt bikes, dual-sports and ATVs.

Which brands?

Name-brand manufacturers, including Honda, Yamaha, Kawasaki, Suzuki, KTM, Husqvarna, Harley-Davidson, Polaris, Can-Am, Arctic Cat, Triumph, Ducati, BMW and Kymco. Not sure about yours? Send a photo and we'll tell you.

Do you buy bikes that don't run?

Yes. Most of what we buy doesn't run. Seized engines, electrical problems, crash damage and missing parts are all fine.

What about a bike that's been sitting for years?

Barn finds, yard bikes and abandoned projects are exactly what we're looking for.

What happens to my bike after you buy it?

We take it apart and send the good parts to riders around the country who need them to keep their own machines running.
