Before you grab the toolbox

Why you shouldn't fix it first

1

We buy for parts

Our offer is built on what the engine, wheels, frame and the rest are worth. A few hundred in repairs usually won't raise it by a few hundred.

2

Repairs snowball

A new battery turns into a carb clean, then a fuel pump, then tires. A bike that's sat for years rarely stops at one problem.

3

Your weekend counts

If you're not going to ride it, every hour in the garage is an hour you don't get back. One photo gets you a number.

Non-runners we buy

Whatever's wrong with it, tell us.

  • Dead battery, sat for years
  • Old gas and gummed-up carbs
  • Seized or locked-up engine
  • Electrical problems nobody could track down
  • Half-finished projects in boxes
  • Barn and yard bikes with flat tires and rust

What we look at

Does the engine turn over?

Try the kickstarter or the starter button. Turning over versus locked solid is the biggest single question.

How long, and where, it sat

Five years in a garage is different from five years under a tree.

Is it all there?

Projects in pieces are fine. Just keep the parts together so we get everything at pickup.

Paperwork

A title in hand is easiest. Lost it? Let's talk. We check the VIN on every bike and don't buy stolen machines.

What to send us for the fastest offer

  1. A photo of each side of the bike
  2. A photo of the VIN on the frame, or the title
  3. The odometer or hour meter, if it still lights up
  4. One sentence on what's wrong and how long it's sat
  5. What town it's in
